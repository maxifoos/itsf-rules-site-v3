# 2. The Prelude

Every match begins with the *prelude*, a process where the teams prepare for matchplay. Players should complete the prelude in a timely manner. Time-outs are not allowed during the prelude. Either team can request a referee during the prelude.

If a referee is present during the prelude they can issue Conduct Violations. The prelude ends when the ready prompt in the first serve is issued, whereupon all matchplay rules are in force.

## 2.1 Evaluate Conditions

Each team is allowed 60 seconds on each side of the table in order to evaluate the equipment and the playing conditions, and if necessary to request table maintenance. Once active play has begun, table maintenance can only be called for a sudden alteration to the table.

During the prelude any object resting on the surface of the cabinet must be removed.

## 2.2 Ball Supply

During the prelude, the teams may agree on the *ball supply*. If they do not agree, the ball supply is a single new ball.

At the beginning of any pause between games, either team may require the existing ball supply to be replaced by a ball supply consisting of a single new ball. The other team must be informed before the replacement is made, but their agreement is not required.

At any other time, the ball supply may be changed only with consent.

:::danger[Rule: Ball Supply Change]
A player must not change the ball in play or modify the ball supply except as permitted above.

**Sanction:** Conduct Violation
:::

## 2.3 Initial Flip

The *initial flip* is typically a coin toss between one player from each team. The winner of the initial flip has the choice of either selecting the table side for the first game, or taking the first kick-off of the match, with the loser of the initial flip receiving the remaining option. Once the winner has made their choice they cannot change their decision.

## 2.4 Warm-up

Both teams must adopt their nominated sides. The players are then allowed to practice on their respective table sides for up to 90 seconds before the first game. See [Practice](./07-switching-positions.md#72-practice).
