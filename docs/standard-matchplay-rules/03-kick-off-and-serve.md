# 3. Kick-off and Serve

The *kick-off* is a specialised serve from the 5-rod.

The first kick-off of the match is performed by the team that gained that option from the initial flip in the prelude. Subsequent kick-offs are performed by the team which conceded the previous point.

If the kick-off is performed by the wrong team and the mistake is discovered before the next controlled transition by the other team, then the ball shall be given to the correct team for the kick-off. Once the other team performs a controlled transition then no protest shall be allowed and play will continue as if no mistake had been made.

The serve is a three step process for putting the ball into play:

- Retrieve and position the ball
- Ready protocol
- Opening transfer

The serve is played from a playfield location in accordance with the tables below.

## 3.1 Retrieve and Position the Ball

If the ball is not on the playfield then it is retrieved and given to the serving player.

If the ball is on the playfield it can be played to the serving rod or placed there by hand.

The serving rod is determined as follows:

| Preceding Event | Serving Rod |
| --- | --- |
| First ball of match | Kick-off rod |
| Point scored, new game | Kick-off rod |
| Dead ball between the 5-rods | 5-rod of the team that played the last kick-off |
| Dead ball behind a 5-rod | 2-rod in the nearest goalie area |
| Ball off Table | 2-rod of the team opposite the one that had the last controlled possession |
| Interrupt | Rod that had the last controlled possession |

In the case of a Rod Possession Award following an infraction the non-offending team has the serve:

| Infraction Award | Non-offending Team's Serving Rod |
| --- | --- |
| 5-rod Rod Possession Award | 5-rod |
| 2-rod Rod Possession Award | 2-rod |
| Continue option | Current rod of controlled possession |
| Replay option | Rod of last controlled possession |
| Penalty shot | 3-rod |

The ball must be positioned at a serve location on the playfield in accordance with the following table:

| Serving Rod | Serving Figure | Serving Location on Playfield |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2-rod | Either figure | Anywhere such that no part of the ball is directly in front of the goalmouth |
| 5-rod | Central figure | Within reach of the central figure |
| 3-rod | Either wing figure | Close enough to either side wall such that a figure could not fit between the wall and the ball |

## 3.2 Ready Protocol

The *ready protocol* ensures that both teams are ready for active play.

The ready protocol can begin when all the players are in position at the table, in control of their rods, and the ball is stopped at a legal playfield location within reach of a legal serving figure.

The player serving the ball asks the opposing player if they are ready for active play to begin by issuing the *ready prompt*. The opposing player is then expected to issue the *ready response* without delay. The serving rod must remain stationary until the ready response is given.

The ready prompt and the ready response must be delivered verbally and audibly using a single unambiguous word, such as "Ready?" and "Ready!".

Active play begins when the ready response is given.

## 3.3 Opening Transfer

After the ready response is given, the player serving the ball must perform an *opening transfer* from the serving figure before the ball can be legally transitioned. The contact that completes the opening transfer can be the one that also performs the transition.

:::danger[Rule: No Opening Transfer]
The player with the serve must not perform a transition or play the ball dead before completing an opening transfer from the serving figure.

**Sanction:**
- First in serve: Replay
- Subsequent in same serve: 5-rod Possession Award
:::
