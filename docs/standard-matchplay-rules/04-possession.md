# 4. Possession

*Possession* relates to which rod, and by extension which player and team, has ownership of the ball at any point in time, including when the ball is due to be served from that rod.

*Active possession* begins when the ready response is given, and during active play when the ball enters a rod area. Active possession ends when the ball leaves a rod area or goes dead in the goalie area. Each active possession is time-limited to 12 seconds.

:::danger[Rule: Possession Time]
A single active possession must not exceed 12 seconds.

**Award:** 5-rod Possession Award
:::

## 4.1 Possession-Time Claim

When a match is not under time control, a player who believes that an opponent's current active possession has clearly exceeded the maximum permitted duration may initiate a player interrupt by saying "Stop!"

The interrupt must be made while the active possession is continuing. The player must state that they are making a possession-time claim, which is then processed using the fact check and resolution process.

If the players agree that the possession time was exceeded, the opposing team receives a 5-Rod Possession Award.

If they disagree, the match should then continue under time control, and the team whose possession was interrupted receives a Replay.

A player should not interrupt active play for a close or uncertain count. A recurring concern about possession time should instead be raised during a break in play, with a request for time control.

<details>
<summary>Refereed Match</summary>

A timekeeping referee will call "Time!" to declare a possession time infraction. In a match with a referee who is not timekeeping, any player can request time control during a break in play.

</details>

## 4.2 Precarious Ball

A ball is *precarious* when it is stopped and any part of the ball is directly in front of the goalmouth and behind the goalkeeper rod, for example if it is:

- overhanging the goal line
- behind the goalkeeper
- pinned to the lip of the goalmouth, a goalpost or the crossbar

A player with a precarious ball may declare "Precarious ball" to initiate a player interrupt at which point active play stops; the player does not have to wait for the possession time to expire. If the ball drops into the goal after the declaration the goal does not count; the ball is retrieved and served from the opposing team's 5-rod.

With both teams observing, the player moves the goalkeeper through its range of movement at the position of the ball to determine whether it is reachable.

- If the ball is reachable by the goalkeeper, it is retrieved and served from the opposing team's 5-rod
- If the ball is unreachable by the goalkeeper, it is a dead ball and is retrieved and served from that team's 2-rod

<details>
<summary>Refereed Match</summary>

The referee determines whether the ball is precarious and supervises the reachability test.

</details>

## 4.3 Match Record

Each team must use the tally markers provided on the table to accurately record its points scored, time-outs taken, and games won. The markers must be updated when each event occurs.

If it is established that a point was legally scored earlier in the current game, but was not marked, the score shall be corrected and the point added, and similarly, if it is established that a time-out was not marked earlier in the current game, the marker shall be corrected.

If it is established that a game was not marked earlier in the match, the game marker shall be corrected.

:::danger[Rule: Tally Marker Usage]
A player must not refuse to use their tally markers to accurately reflect the state of the match.

**Sanction:** Conduct Violation
:::

## 4.4 Point Scored

A goal is scored when the ball completely crosses the goal line, regardless of whether it subsequently exits the goal.

If the goal is legally scored then a point is awarded to the appropriate team, who must mark it on the relevant tally marker.

An own-goal scored as a result of an infraction by the same team will effectively count, because the non-offending team has the option to Continue play from the current location of the ball.

If it is established that a point was legally scored earlier in the current game, but was not marked, the score shall be corrected and the point added.

:::danger[Rule: Illegal Point Mark]
A player must not intentionally mark, or knowingly have marked, a point for a goal that was not legally scored.

**Sanction:** Conduct Violation
:::
