# 5. Time-out

A *time-out* extends the duration of a pause by up to 30 seconds.

Either team can call a time-out between points, between games and during the serve that follows one of these pauses.

Each team has two time-outs per game. A time-out called between games counts towards the total in the following game.

A player calls a time-out by using both hands to make a T-shaped gesture above the playfield, and simultaneously announcing "Time-out!".

If the time-out is called during a pause then the pause is extended and the additional time follows any time remaining in the pause.

If the time-out is called during the serve that follows a pause then it reopens and extends the preceding pause.

The team with the serve loses the option to call a time-out when they issue the ready prompt, and the opposing team loses their option to call a time-out when they give the ready response.

The permissions and restrictions of the original pause continue to apply during the extended pause.

:::note[Refereed Match]
If a timekeeping referee is at the table then they will time the pause between games, and any pause extended or reopened by a time-out. The referee will place the timer on the top of the table for the players to view. They will also announce the time remaining at appropriate intervals. At the end of the pause the referee will instruct the teams to "Play!" and the player with possession must put the ball into play with a serve. Delays by either team are subject to the [Delay of Game](./08-delay-of-game.md) rule.
:::
