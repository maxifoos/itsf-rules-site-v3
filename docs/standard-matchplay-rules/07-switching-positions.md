# 7. Switching Positions

In standard doubles the players on a team are allowed to switch their playing positions between points, between games and before and after a penalty shot. The team with the kick-off must assume their respective playing positions before the other team.

Both teams are free to switch positions until the ready protocol has begun. However, once the ready prompt is issued the offensive team is no longer allowed to switch positions, and when the ready response is given the defensive team is no longer allowed to switch positions.

It is an infraction to switch positions during active play. At other times switching positions is disallowed but not penalised.

Switching positions must be completed without delay.

:::danger[Rule: Illegal Position Switch]
The players in a doubles team must not switch positions during active play.

**Sanction:** Conduct Violation
:::

## 7.1 Switching Table Sides

As soon as possible after the first game played, either team may request to switch table sides, in which case the teams must switch sides after each game. However, the teams can subsequently agree to stop switching sides, in which case after doing so they must remain on the same sides for the rest of the match. Side switching must be performed without delay.

<details>
<summary>Refereed Match</summary>

Upon completion of the first game played the referee will prompt the teams with the option to switch sides.

</details>

## 7.2 Practice

*Practice* is defined as repeated transfers or transitions which are performed with control. Playing the ball into position for a serve is not practice.

Players are allowed to practice between games on their designated table side for the next game, and during any break that has exceeded 10 minutes in duration.

If either team asks for exclusive use of the table to practice, the teams shall share the remaining time equally, with the team that won the preceding game taking the first half of the remaining time, and the team that conceded the preceding game taking the second half of the remaining time.

:::danger[Rule: Illegal Practice]
A player must not practice between points or during an interrupt that has not yet exceeded 10 minutes in duration.

**Sanction:** Conduct Violation
:::
