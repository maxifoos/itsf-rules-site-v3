# 10. Spinning

:::danger[Rule: Free Spinning]
During active play a player must not:

- accelerate the rotation of a spinning rod that is not under control
- allow the continued rotation of a spinning rod that is not under control

**Sanction:** Conduct Violation
:::

:::danger[Rule: Pre-Contact Spin]
It is illegal to transition the ball by rotating the rod such that it enters the *strike zone* more than once before striking the ball.

**Award:** 5-rod Possession Award
:::
