# 11. Passing

A *pass* is a controlled transition between two rods on the same team which are separated by at least one rod on the opposing team. The pass is successfully completed only if and when the receiving rod gains controlled possession of the ball or performs a controlled transition.

A *direct pass* is a pass from the 5-rod to the 3-rod or from either goalie rod to the 5-rod, where there is no contact with the opposing team's intervening rod, and where the ball does not become reachable by an opposing rod forward of the receiving rod.

There are some restrictions on when it is legal to direct pass the ball, however, it is legal to direct pass the ball after a transfer if the ball is moving, unless the ball has been wing bounced off the side wall more than twice.

There are no restrictions on passes:

- Where there is contact on an intervening rod of the opposing team
- Where the ball transitions beyond the receiving rod but is then subsequently caught on that rod
- From a goalie rod to the 3-rod
- Where the transition is backwards

## 11.1 Wing Bounce Passing

A *wing bounce* occurs when the ball is bounced off a side wall with the wing figure closest to that wall followed by a further contact with the same wing figure.

Players are not allowed to perform a direct pass with the wing figure after three or more consecutive wing bounces.

The restriction is cleared by any transfer, by playing the ball off the opposite side wall, and in the goalie area by playing the ball off the back wall.

Wing bounces are not counted until after the opening transfer is complete, and during active play wing bounces are not counted until controlled possession is achieved.

## 11.2 Direct Pass Restrictions

:::danger[Rule: Stopped Ball Pass]
A player must not perform a direct pass on a ball which has stopped moving if there is any feint or hesitation before the transition. Performing a transfer or playing the ball off the back wall removes the restriction.

**Award:** 5-rod Possession Award
:::

:::danger[Rule: Pinned Ball Pass]
A player must not perform a direct pass on the ball after it has been pinned. Performing a transfer or playing the ball off the back wall removes the restriction. The pass is legal if an attempt to pin the ball results in an inadvertent transition.

**Award:** 5-rod Possession Award
:::

:::danger[Rule: Double Contact Pass]
After the initial contact of the active possession, if there is a contact with any part of a figure other than the side, then a player must not subsequently perform a direct pass with the same figure. Performing a transfer or playing the ball off the back wall removes the restriction.

**Award:** 5-rod Possession Award
:::

:::danger[Rule: Adjustment Pass]
A player must not perform a direct pass on a ball that has been moved laterally in one direction and then in the opposite direction as part of a single continuous contact with the front or back of a figure. Performing a transfer or playing the ball off the back wall removes the restriction.

**Award:** 5-rod Possession Award
:::

:::danger[Rule: Overbounced Wing Pass]
A player must not perform a direct pass with the wing figure after playing three or more consecutive wing bounces. Performing a transfer or playing the ball off the opposite side wall or the back wall removes the restriction.

**Award:** 5-rod Possession Award
:::
