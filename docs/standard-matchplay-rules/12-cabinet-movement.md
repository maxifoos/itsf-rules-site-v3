# 12. Cabinet Movement

*Cabinet Movement* is motion of the top of the cabinet resulting from a player's action during active play.

Cabinet Movement is classified into one of three levels:

| Classification | Cabinet Movement |
| --- | --- |
| Level 1 | A slight vibration of the cabinet which can be felt but may not be visible |
| Level 2 | A visible vibration of the cabinet which can be both felt and seen |
| Level 3 | A strong shake or movement of the cabinet which can be both felt and seen |

## 12.1 Cabinet Movement Infractions

:::danger[Rule: Cabinet Movement During Borderline Possession]
A player must not cause any level of cabinet movement if the ball is overhanging the goal line, or is just out of reach of any rod and is stopped or moving slowly.

**Award:** 5-Rod Possession Award
:::

:::danger[Rule: Level 2 Cabinet Movement During Controlled Possession]
A player must not cause Level 2 cabinet movement while the opposing team has controlled possession of the ball.

**Award:** Reset
:::

:::danger[Rule: Level 3 Cabinet Movement During Controlled Possession]
A player must not cause a Level 3 cabinet movement while the opposing team has controlled possession of the ball.

**Award:** Replay

**Sanction:** Escalate under the [Escalation Process](./16-referee-and-escalation.md#161-escalation-process)
:::

:::danger[Rule: Level 3 Cabinet Movement During Attacking Play]
A player must not cause a Level 3 cabinet movement during the opposing team's attacking play if that play ends with a contact but not a controlled possession on a rod belonging to the attacking team.

**Award:** Contact Rod Possession Award
:::

## 12.2 Attacking Play

An *attacking play* begins with a transition from a rod that has a controlled possession, and includes any subsequent contacts with a side wall, back wall or goalpost. An attacking play ends when:

- The ball makes contact with a figure or rod on either team
- The attacking team achieves controlled possession of the ball
- The ball hits a goal crossbar
- Active play ends (i.e. goal scored, dead ball, ball off table, interrupt to play)
