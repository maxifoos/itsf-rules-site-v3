# 13. Table and Ball Interference

If necessary, the table may be repositioned with consent during a suitable break. During active play it is illegal to lift or slide the table.

:::danger[Rule: Table Displacement]
During active play, a player must not cause the feet of the table to lift, slide or otherwise move from their position.

**Award:** First Reachable Rod Possession Award

**Sanction:** Escalate under the [Escalation Process](./16-referee-and-escalation.md#161-escalation-process)
:::

## 13.1 Illegal Influence

It is illegal to influence the motion of the ball during active play by any means other than direct contact with a figure, for example by:

- Creating an air draft, e.g. by blowing on the ball or spinning a nearby rod
- Touching the ball by reaching into the playing area
- Introducing a foreign object into the playing area

However, a ball that is stopped and in contact with the vertical part of the side wall is a *trapped ball*. It is legal to bump the rod of possession against the opposite side wall in order to free a trapped ball.

:::danger[Rule: Illegal Influence]
It is illegal to influence the motion of the ball during active play by any means other than direct contact with a figure. If the action prevents the ball from entering the offending team's goal, the opposing team is awarded a point.

Exception: It is legal to bump the rod of possession against the opposite side wall in order to free a trapped ball.

**Award:** Next Rod Possession Award, possible Point Award

**Sanction:** Conduct Violation
:::

## 13.2 Reaching Into the Playing Area

If during active play, a player wishes to reach into the playing area, they must issue a player interrupt and gain consent. Outside active play, a player may reach into the playing area without consent provided that they do not touch the ball.

A player may touch a ball in the playing area with consent, and when allowed by the rules to retrieve or position it.

:::danger[Rule: Reaching In]
During active play, a player must not reach into the playing area without consent.

**Sanction:** Conduct Violation
:::
