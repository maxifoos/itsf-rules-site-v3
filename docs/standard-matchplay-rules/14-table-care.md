# 14. Table Care

## 14.1 Routine Maintenance

The following activities are considered to be routine maintenance:

- Clearing debris from the playfield
- Lubricating the rods
- Tightening a loose handle or fixing a loose grip

Routine maintenance is always permitted outside active play without the need for consent, and provided that it does not cause delay.

Players are allowed to briefly wipe or remove debris from the playfield provided that no substance is used in the process.

Players may lubricate their own team's rods, but must be aware of the rule against Contamination. Players must also avoid introducing lubricant where it can pose risk to players, for example on the handles or on the venue floor.

The tournament director may require the use of specific lubricant products, and may mandate specific requirements and restrictions for their use.

## 14.2 Table Modification

Where the table is designed to easily change handles, players are allowed to replace their handles. This can be done during any pause provided that it is completed within the time limits.

It is illegal to modify the playing characteristics of the table, or to contaminate any part of the table with a foreign substance.

It is legal to correct a defect to a table component provided this is done prior to the initial flip with the knowledge of the other team, or subsequently with consent.

Players are permitted to use a substance to improve handle grip provided that it does not get into the playing area or onto the rods or the ball. Before changing table sides, a player must remove any substance or material they have applied to the handles.

:::danger[Rule: Illegal Modification]
A player must not modify the playing characteristics of the table or the ball, or use any device or object to restrict the movement of a rod.

**Sanction:** Conduct Violation
:::

:::danger[Rule: Contamination]
A player must not use any substance to clean the ball, the playfield or any other gameplay component, or allow them to become contaminated by any substance.

**Sanction:**
- First in match: Warning
- Subsequent in match: Penalty Shot
:::

## 14.3 Table Maintenance

If there is a sudden alteration to a table component, fixture, or attachment such as table lighting, then the appropriate table maintenance should be performed. If the problem cannot be resolved by the players, a table maintenance request should be made at the tournament desk.

After the problem is resolved, the ball shall be served by the player with possession. However, if the sudden alteration was a disruption which materially impacted the outcome of a play, then the disrupted play shall not stand, and the ball shall be given to the rod which last had controlled possession for a Replay.

The table may be levelled during the prelude. Once active play has started a player may only request to level the table during a pause.

Either team may request that the table be levelled, but if the teams disagree about whether levelling is necessary, they should request a referee to resolve the situation. If levelling is required, the players may either agree to do it themselves or request table maintenance.
