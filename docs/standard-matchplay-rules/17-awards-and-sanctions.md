# 17. Awards and Sanctions

The proscriptive rules specify either an award or a sanction, or both, to be applied in the event of an infraction. An award is a compensation given to the non-offending team, while a sanction is a penalty given to the offending team.

## 17.1 Awards

An award is a compensation given to the non-offending team that specifies the rod to which the ball is awarded, from which they will restart play. Awards can also be used as compromises when resolving a disagreement.

A team entitled to a possession award may instead choose to Continue from the current location of the ball.

**Continue**: Play continues from the current location of the ball.

The Continue option may be exercised explicitly when an infraction claim is being processed, or implicitly if the non-offending team continues active play, in which case they lose the right to declare the infraction after performing a controlled transition following their next controlled possession.

**Contact Rod Possession Award**: During or immediately following the infraction, if the ball makes contact with a figure or rod of the non-offending team, then the ball is awarded to the non-offending team on the rod where the contact occurred.

**First Reachable Rod Possession Award**: During or immediately following the infraction, the ball is awarded to the first rod of the non-offending team from which the ball becomes reachable.

**Return**: The ball is returned to the rod where the infraction occurred.

**Replay**: A serve from the rod of last controlled possession.

**5-Rod Possession Award**: The ball is awarded to the non-offending team for a serve from the 5-rod. The option to Continue from the current location of the ball is also available to the non-offending team.

**2-Rod Possession Award**: The ball is awarded to the non-offending team for a serve from the 2-rod. The option to Continue from the current location of the ball is also available to the non-offending team.

**Next Rod Possession Award**: The ball is awarded to the non-offending team on their next rod in the ball's direction of travel at the time of the infraction.

**Reset**: If the referee announces "Reset!" then the possession clock and the wing bounce count are reset.

**Point Award**: The non-offending team is awarded a point. The offending team then has the kick-off.

## 17.2 Sanctions

A sanction is a penalty given to the offending team following an infraction.

**Warning**: A warning indicates that a subsequent infraction of a similar nature will carry a more severe penalty.

**Forced Time-out**: The offending team is required to take a time-out in order to process the reason for their delay. However, if the delay occurred when the offending team would not legally have been permitted to call a time-out, or if they have no time-outs remaining, then the offending team has committed a Conduct Violation.

**Penalty Shot**: The ball is awarded to the non-offending team's 3-rod for a single attempt to score a goal against one defender from the offending team; see the [Penalty Shot](./18-penalty-shot.md) chapter for the full procedure.

**Forfeit Game**: The offending team forfeit the game.

**Forfeit Match**: The offending team forfeit the match.

**Conduct Violation**: A player may inform the offending player that their conduct is prohibited and ask them to stop. If necessary, the player may request a referee to resolve the matter; see [Penalty Cards](./16-referee-and-escalation.md#167-penalty-cards) for how a referee enforces this.
