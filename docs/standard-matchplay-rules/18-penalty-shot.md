# 18. Penalty Shot

For a Penalty Shot the ball is awarded to the 3-rod of the non-offending team for a single attempt to score a goal against the offending team.

In standard doubles, the non-offending team chooses one of their players to be the penalty taker, and then the offending team chooses one of their players to be the defender. The other two players leave the table.

The ball is put into play with a 3-rod serve by the penalty taker and the penalty is taken. During the Penalty Shot all matchplay rules are in force.

If the penalty taker's transition results in a legally scored goal, then the Penalty Shot is completed successfully and the point is marked by the penalty taker.

The Penalty Shot is completed unsuccessfully if any of the following occur:

- The penalty taker commits a rule infraction with a sanction other than a Warning
- The transition goes backwards
- The ball goes dead
- After the penalty taker's transition:
  - the ball enters and then leaves the defender's goalie area
  - the ball stops moving
  - the ball goes off table

After the Penalty Shot is complete the players return to the table and play continues as follows:

- Either team may switch their playing positions
- If the goal was legally scored then the offending team has the next kick-off
- If the goal was not scored then the ball is served from the infraction location, or if no infraction location can be identified, a 5-rod Possession Award is given

:::danger[Rule: Penalty Shot Limit]
A team must not be charged with three Penalty Shots in a single game.

**Sanction:** Forfeit Game
:::
