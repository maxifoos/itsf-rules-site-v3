# 20. Definitions

Defined terms are used consistently throughout the rules and are collected here, grouped by topic.

## 20.1 Table Components

**2-rod area**: The area where the ball is reachable by the 2-rod.

**3-rod area**: The area where the ball is reachable by the 3-rod.

**5-rod area**: The area where the ball is reachable by the 5-rod.

**Ball supply**: The specific ball or set of balls that will be used during the match.

**Cabinet**: Any part of the frame of the table which is external to the playing area.

**Figure**: A playing figure on any of the rods, used to control the ball during play.

**Goal line**: The edge of the playfield in the goalmouth.

**Goalie area**: The area combining the goalkeeper area, the 2-rod area, and the space between them.

**Goalie rod**: Either the goalkeeper rod or the adjacent 2-rod.

**Goalkeeper**: The figure on the goalkeeper rod which covers the goalmouth.

**Goalkeeper area**: The area where the ball is reachable by the goalkeeper rod.

**Goalkeeper rod**: The rod closest to the goal.

**New ball**: An unused ball of the approved type obtained from the tournament venue.

**Playfield**: The internal surface of the table on which the ball is played.

**Playing area**: Any part of the internal volume of the table from the playfield up to the height of the side of the cabinet, excluding any tally markers.

**Rod area**: A 3-rod area, a 5-rod area, or a goalie area.

**Side wall**: One of the two internal vertical surfaces that bound the sides of the playing area, including any side strip that runs along each side of the wall.

**Strike zone**: The zone that includes all rotation angles of a rod between a back-pin position and a front-pin position.

**Tally marker**: A table fixture that records the number of points scored, games won or time-outs taken by a team.

## 20.2 Match Periods

**Time-limited**: A period in a match that must not exceed a specified maximum duration, i.e. active possession, between points, between games.

**Active play**: A period of live play when the ball is reachable or moving either between the rods or above the playfield.

**Pause**: A time-limited break, either between points, between games.

**Between games**: A pause that begins when a point is scored that ends the game.

**Between points**: A pause that begins when a point is scored that doesn't end the game.

**Interrupt**: An exceptional break in active play: a dead ball, ball off table, a player interrupt or a disruption.

**Player interrupt**: An exceptional break in active play initiated by one of the players.

**Referee interrupt**: An exceptional break in active play initiated by the referee.

**Disruption**: An exceptional interrupt to matchplay which is not directly caused by a player at the table.

**Medical break**: An interrupt that allows a player to recover from a transient medical issue or injury.

## 20.3 Player Actions

**Initial flip**: A procedure that determines which team has the choice of selecting the table side for the first game, or taking the first kick-off of the match.

**Disturbance**: An unnecessary or excessive player action performed outside the playing area during active play.

**Deception**: An attempt by a player to cause the opposing team to commit to an unfavourable action.

**Delay**: Stalling or performing unnecessary activities during a break in active play.

**Consent**: In an unrefereed match, consent is given by the agreement of the other team. In a refereed match, consent is given by the referee. During active play gaining consent requires a player interrupt.

**Under control**: A rod is under control when the player is holding that rod's handle using any part of their arm from the elbow down to the fingers.

**Time-out**: An extension to the pause between points or between games of up to 30 seconds, or a reopening of that pause if called during the serve.

## 20.4 Possession

**Possession**: The rod, and by extension the player and the team, which has ownership of the ball at any point in time, including when the ball is due to be served from that rod.

**Active possession**: A period of active play during which the ball is not dead within a rod area.

**Controlled possession**: An active possession during which a stopped or moving ball is retained on a rod, regardless of contact with a figure.

**Opposing player**: The player who controls the rod that directly opposes the current rod of possession.

**Reachable**: A ball is reachable from a rod if the foot of any figure on that rod can contact it without flexing the rod.

**Unreachable**: A ball that is not reachable by any rod.

## 20.5 Ball States

**Stopped ball**: A ball under controlled possession which is not moving and has not been immediately transitioned.

**Moving ball**: A ball in a state of motion, whether through linear movement or rotation.

**Dead ball**: A stopped ball in the playing area that is not reachable by any rod.

**Pinned ball**: A stopped or moving ball that is pressed to the playfield by a figure.

**Ball off table**: A ball that leaves the playing area and strikes a foreign object either on the cabinet or outside the table, or which comes to rest on the top of the cabinet.

**Held ball**: A ball which is stopped under controlled possession as soon as possible in order to process an interrupt.

**Current location**: The current location of the ball, i.e. under possession by a specific rod, dead, off table, or in a goal.

**Precarious ball**: A stopped ball any part of which is behind the goalkeeper rod and directly in front of the goalmouth.

**Trapped ball**: A reachable ball that is stopped and in contact with the vertical part of the side wall.

## 20.6 Serving the Ball

**Serve**: The process for putting the ball into play from a specific rod.

**Kick-off**: A serve at the central 5-rod figure by the team which conceded the previous goal, or if there is no score by the team which gained the kick-off from the initial flip.

**Ready protocol**: Part of the process for serving the ball; the ready prompt and the ready response.

**Ready prompt**: The first part of the ready protocol where the player serving the ball asks the opposing player if they are ready for active play to begin.

**Ready response**: The second part of the ready protocol where the opposing player states that they are ready for active play to begin.

**Serving figure**: During a serve, the figure at which the first contact of the opening transfer is made.

**Opening transfer**: During the serve, a transfer from the serving figure to any other figure on the same rod.

## 20.7 Playing the Ball

**Contact**: When the ball touches a figure on a rod, or any part of a rod that is internal to the playing area.

**Move**: A contact that causes a stopped ball to move, or a moving ball to change speed or direction.

**Transfer**: A move followed by a contact on another figure in the same rod area.

**Transition**: A move that causes the ball to become unreachable by the rod of active possession and reachable by another rod.

**Controlled transition**: A transition performed by a controlled rotation of the rod, or any transition which follows a controlled possession.

**Pass**: A controlled transition from one rod to another rod on the same team, where the passing and receiving rods are separated by at least one rod on the opposing team; the pass is completed if the receiving rod either achieves controlled possession or performs a controlled transition.

**Direct pass**: A pass from the 5-rod to the 3-rod or from either goalie rod to the 5-rod, where there is no contact with the opposing team's intervening rod, and where the ball does not become reachable by an opposing rod forward of the receiving rod.

**Wing bounce**: Bouncing the ball with a wing figure off the closest side wall followed by a further contact with the same wing figure.
